# Awesome Memoir Writing Tools [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI and writing tools for turning real memories, interviews, and family stories into a finished memoir.

Tools for every stage of a memoir or life story book: gathering memories and records, recording and transcribing interviews, shaping a period of life into chapters, drafting with AI, revising, and producing a printed or digital book. A memoir is about real people, so AI drafts should stay inside the material you supply, mark uncertain dates and dialogue instead of inventing them, and respect other people's privacy. Every chapter still needs the author's review.

⭐ = recommended pick for memoir writers.

## AI Memoir Drafting Suites

Long-form tools that take your memories, notes, or an outline and help draft full chapters.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/book-generators/memoir) - Memoir planner and book drafter that shapes your supplied memories and outline into chapters (5,000 to 120,000 words), with prompts that keep uncertain details marked rather than invented.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI drafting, outlines, and chapter organization for fiction and nonfiction.
- [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/) - AI prose suite with brainstorming, rewriting, and describe tools that also work for creative nonfiction scenes.
- [Memoirji](https://www.memoirji.com/) - Chat-based AI memoir writer on WhatsApp that turns your answers into a PDF life story.

## Guided Life Story Services

Prompt-driven services, often given as gifts, where a parent or grandparent answers questions and receives a keepsake book.

- ⭐ [Storyworth](https://welcome.storyworth.com/) - Weekly story prompts by email, collected into a printed keepsake book after a year.
- [Remento](https://www.remento.co/) - Voice-first life story book where recorded answers are turned into written stories with no typing required.
- [Meminto](https://meminto.com/) - Question-based Life Story Book that family members answer at their own pace.
- [Tell Mel](https://www.tellmel.ai/) - AI interviewer that phones aging parents and writes each call up as a life story chapter.
- [Storii](https://storii.com/) - Phone prompt service that preserves a loved one's spoken stories as a keepsake audiobook.

## Professional Memoir Ghostwriting

When you want a human writer to interview you and write the book.

- [StoryTerrace](https://www.storyterrace.com/) - Memoir and biography service pairing you with a professional ghostwriter and printing the result.
- [Reedsy Ghostwriters](https://reedsy.com/freelancers/ghostwriters) - Marketplace of vetted freelance ghostwriters, including memoir specialists.

## Interviews, Recording and Transcription

Capturing your own voice or a relative's stories so the exact phrasing survives into the draft.

- ⭐ [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) - Live transcription with speaker labels for recorded family interviews.
- [StoryCorps App](https://storycorps.org/participate/storycorps-app/) - Free app for recording guided conversations with loved ones, with question lists.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) - Transcript-based audio editor for cleaning up long interview recordings.
- [Rev](https://www.rev.com/) - AI and human transcription for interview audio.
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) - Open-source speech recognition model for transcribing recordings locally.
- [MacWhisper](https://goodsnooze.gumroad.com/l/macwhisper) - Mac app that runs Whisper on-device, useful for private family recordings.

## Memory Gathering, Photos and Family Research

Collecting the dated evidence, photos, and records that anchor what you remember.

- [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/) - Journal app for capturing memories, photos, and dated entries as raw memoir material.
- [Photomyne](https://www.photomyne.com/) - Phone app for scanning, restoring, and organizing old family photos.
- [Google PhotoScan](https://www.google.com/photos/scan/) - Free phone scanner for printed photos with glare removal.
- [Legacybox](https://www.legacybox.com/) - Mail-in service that digitizes tapes, film, slides, and photos.
- [FamilySearch](https://www.familysearch.org/) - Free genealogy records and family tree for checking dates, places, and names.
- [MyHeritage](https://www.myheritage.com/) - Genealogy platform with historical records and family tree tools.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) - Digital library of books, newspapers, and web pages for period detail.

## Structure and Timelines

Choosing a period, question, or relationship and arranging scenes around it.

- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/) - Long-form writing app with binder and corkboard for arranging scenes and research.
- [Aeon Timeline](https://www.aeontimeline.com/) - Timeline software for lining up life events, people, and ages.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - Local Markdown notes with links, handy for a private memory archive.

## Editing and Manuscript Rewrite

Revising a full draft, or reshaping a memoir you have already written.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite that takes an existing Word, Markdown, text, or HTML draft and restructures, retones, translates, or expands it while leaving the original untouched.
- ⭐ [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - Style and manuscript reports for repetition, pacing, and consistency.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - Readability checker that flags dense sentences and passive voice.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Grammar, tone, and clarity suggestions.
- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Manuscript critique with analysis of pacing, dialogue, and word choice.

## Formatting and Export

Turning the manuscript into files for readers, printers, and ebook stores.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Downloads of the finished memoir as Word, EPUB, and Markdown.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Cross-platform book formatter for ebook and print interiors.
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) - Mac book formatting software with polished interior styles.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio with EPUB and print-ready PDF export.
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) - Open-source converter between Markdown, Word, EPUB, and PDF.
- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) - Open-source ebook manager and converter for checking EPUB files.

## Printing and Publishing

From a handful of family copies to a public release.

- [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/) - Print-on-demand for single copies or small family runs, plus retail distribution.
- [Blurb](https://www.blurb.com/) - Print service for photo-rich books and keepsake editions.
- [BookBaby](https://www.bookbaby.com/) - Self-publishing service with printing, ebook distribution, and editing packages.
- [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/) - Kindle and paperback self-publishing on Amazon.
- [Draft2Digital](https://www.draft2digital.com/) - Free ebook and print distribution to Apple Books, Kobo, libraries, and other stores beyond Amazon.

## Covers and Titles

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Design templates for covers, photo inserts, and family announcement cards.
- [BookBrush](https://bookbrush.com/) - Author graphics studio for covers and 3D mockups.
